## Pass 4 — scripts/board-core.mjs — three answered, two declined

ANSWERED in 5b6feb7:

- [P1] `compareNowProjection` checked neither focus membership nor focused-first order, so
  `{points:[1], focusPoint:2}` passed the fail-closed preflight while the render's own
  ordering rule was broken. Both halves of the invariant read the focus now and name it
  (`focusUnrepresented`, `focusMisplaced`); the Stop side reads the same fields, so the two
  cannot block on different things.

- [P2] The next section boundary was an unvalidated `indexOf(SECT_OPEN)`: authored prose
  carrying the bare token ended the section early, and a section never closed before the
  next one swallowed the rest of the document. A real section opener is recognised by its
  own `<summary><h2>`, an unclosed section refuses by name, and the newline-led spelling of
  the closer is preferred without being demanded.

- The refusal now carries its CAUSE. Missing, doubled, orphaned and unclosed are four
  different repairs, and one generic "heading is missing" sent every one of them looking
  for a heading that was there.

DECLINED, with the reason recorded rather than averaged:

  authored prose disappears before the comparison can see the loss — the renderer must
  refuse or restore it." DECLINED: the point's own final state orders that removal — "it
  CREATES a stub card for a point that has none and REMOVES one whose point is no longer in

  still in flight ("AN EXISTING CARD'S PROSE SURVIVES THE RENDER"). Building the refusal
