# Villager garment construction — merged plan, revision 6, stage 1 (Fable 5.1)

Answers GPT-6 Astra's round-6 break check (docs/garment-check-6-1347.md, B1) for stage 1 only and replaces docs/garment-merge-r5-1347.md in full; revision 6 stands alone. Stage scope is unchanged from revisions 3, 4 and 5: gate removal, before pictures, outfit enumeration and rank table, the body pre-split, the shell builder, and the migration of trousers, breastCloth, veil, neckBeads-neck, neckBeads-chest and waistBeads. Column bones stay at the start of stage 3.

One change this revision makes: the waist-beads region excludes the arms like every other trunk shell, its boundary rows are the boundary of that restricted face set, and a selftest holds the region to one connected component at the waist (B1). The standing mechanisms are as revision 5 left them: the export handoff in pipeline.py forwards the shells; region membership is not a weight test but a predicate on three immutable per-vertex region scalars (height, summed arm weight, summed leg weight per side) computed before the split; the split tool interpolates those scalars along the cut edge and sets the cut scalar to its level value exactly, and classification never reads the refitted skin weights.

Acceptance standard, applied throughout: visibly convincing clothing at 3 m and 7 m in the port city and the village, judged in pictures on both backends; measured numbers are detectors with a named response, not a proof of zero penetration.

## 1. Round-6 objection answer

- **B1 (waist beads lack the arm exclusion)** FIXED. Revision 5's region table gave waistBeads-waist the predicate `y` in [hip + 0.013 H, hip + 0.027 H] alone. The two height cuts at hip + 0.013 H and hip + 0.027 H run over the whole body, and in the A-pose body the hands hang beside the thighs at that height, so the band's level sets also loop round each hand or wrist, those hand faces satisfy the height predicate, they would be cut from the body and drawn as bead-coloured bands on the limbs. The legacy builder's `girdle` (garments.py) rings the trunk with `B.arm < 0.3` and never touched the hands. Revision 6 adds `arm` ≤ 0.2 to the waist-beads predicate, the same bound every other trunk shell uses. The arm contour cut at 0.2 is already restricted to `y` in [hem, neck + 0.002 H], which contains the band, so no new cut is needed and every band face lies on one side of the contour by construction. The hand and wrist cross-sections carry arm scalar 1 and fall out; the hips skin carries arm scalar 0 and stays. Boundary rows are defined for every shell as the boundary loops of its region face set, not as the whole-body level sets, so the waist-beads rim strips lie on the trunk loop of each height cut and nothing is emitted round the hands. A pipeline selftest on the real body asserts that the waistBeads-waist face set is exactly one connected component (faces joined across shared edges), that its boundary is exactly two closed loops, that every face in it has arm scalar 0 at all three corners, and that no body face with a corner at arm scalar 0.2 or above carries the bit. Picture acceptance adds waist beads alone, walk, side-on, judged at the hands and wrists, and "beads on a wrist or hand" joins the judged list.

Revision 5's answers to round-5 B1 (export handoff), B2 (bare seat) and B3 (region labels from refitted weights) stand unchanged and are embodied in the plan below.

**Defect found in revision 5 by this merger.**

- **D10 Waist-beads region.** Revision 5 wrote the arm exclusion into five trunk shells and omitted it from the sixth, and described the rim rows as whole-body level sets, which also loop round the hands at the girdle and the waist (B1). Both are fixed by the predicate row and the boundary-row definition below.

## 2. The stage-1 plan

**Before pictures.** Unchanged: one forced outfit per acceptance row, 3 m and 7 m, both backends, village and port city; the dress sheets under verification/villager-body/ are the pipeline-side before record.

**Outfit enumeration.** Unchanged: the implementing session lists every generated (form, wear) pair and co-worn combination from `appearanceFor` and records it here; equal-rank overlaps are resolved by inserting a rank, never reordering or forbidding.

**Rank table (stage 1).** Offset is base plus rank times step, both from balance config, defaults 3 mm and 3 mm; a shell may override its base (breastCloth's named response).

| Rank | Garments |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | hairBag-crown (stage 5) |
| 1 | neckBeads-neck, breastCloth-chest, loinFlap and girdleTails bands (stage 3) |
| 2 | trousers-waist, neckBeads-chest, apron band, skirtShort, skirtKnee, wrapLong-waist (stage 3) |

Rigid pieces have no rank: cap, headband, headRing, topknot, turban, limbRings, babySling. Hair cap and eyes are pipeline-only.

**Region scalars.** After `build_body` and before any cut, body.py stores `body['region'] = {'y': pos[:, 1], 'arm': W[:, arm bones].sum(1), 'leg.L': ..., 'leg.R': ...}` from the full-width `W`: `y` the rest height, `arm` the sum of the six arm bone columns (as garments.py's `Body.arm`), `leg.L` and `leg.R` the sum of thigh, shin, foot and toe per side (as `Body.leg`; kept for stage 3's leg-split skirts, unused by stage 1). These are the only inputs to region classification. They are extended by the split and never recomputed; `arm` and `leg.*` are not re-read from `W` after the split. Heights come from `landmarks(body['joints'], H)` as in garments.py, H the stature from config. The hem is knee − 0.5 (knee − ankle), knee being `shin.L` and ankle `foot.L`; the body is X-symmetric, so one height serves both legs.

**Body pre-split.** A generic split tool in body.py cuts the decimated body along the level set of a region scalar at a value c, optionally restricted to faces inside a height interval (so a shoulder contour is cut once and a short band's rows cut only where they can exist). On every crossed edge it inserts one vertex at the crossing parameter t with position, every morph delta, every region scalar and the full weight vector interpolated at t; the cut scalar is then set to c exactly, so the vertex is on its level set in the classification scalars by assignment, not by arithmetic; the weights go through `top4` and then the B1 refit from revision 3 (non-negative least squares over the four bones, sum one, over the rest pose, the design pose and the plus and minus 45 and 90 degree rotations about two axes per carried bone), stored back into the full-width `W` with four nonzero entries so export.py's `top4` is the identity on them. The skin weights may move freely in weight space and are read only by skinning. No snap fallback: the vertex stays on its edge whatever the error. Each split triangle becomes two or three with no hanging vertex; `tri_uv` is interpolated per split corner. `body['map']` is extended by `surface_map` on the inserted positions against `basis_full`; morph deltas of inserted vertices are the edge interpolation of their endpoints' deltas, exact at every corner because the inserted point is the same edge point in every corner shape. The cuts, in order, with the whole body cut unless an interval is given:

- `y` at girdle (hip + 0.06 H); `y` at hip + 0.013 H and hip + 0.027 H (waist beads band)
- `y` at hem knee − 0.5 (knee − ankle) (trousers; one height, both legs)
- `y` at chestTop and at waist − 0.01 H (breast cloth); `y` at chestTop − 0.06 H and neck − 0.008 H (chest collar); `y` at neck + 0.002 H (neck band top); `y` at neck − 0.025 H and chin + 0.055 H (veil)
- `arm` at 0.2 (the arm boundary) restricted to faces with `y` in [hem, neck + 0.002 H], which contains every stage-1 region that excludes the arms, the waist beads band included; the contour is one closed loop per shoulder plus nothing elsewhere, because arm weight is zero on trunk and leg skin away from the shoulders and one on the hands that hang beside the thighs

Every body triangle gets a 32-bit region mask, one bit per stage-1 shell, set when all three vertices satisfy that shell's region predicate on `body['region']`: a closed height interval on `y` and, where stated, `arm` at most 0.2. Because every inserted vertex carries the level value exactly, each face lies on exactly one side of every contour and the predicate labels it consistently; the refit cannot unlabel it. Each shell's boundary rows are the boundary edges of its region face set (edges with exactly one face in the set), chained into closed loops of body vertex indices in loop order. They are derived from the restricted region, never from the whole-body level set: a height cut that also loops round a hand contributes only its trunk loop to a shell whose region excludes the arms. The face region masks, each shell's boundary rows and its rank ship in `scene.extras.villager.shells`. The clip-frame regression over every inserted vertex and every split original triangle runs in the garments step as revision 3 describes, reported per edge against 1 mm and 3 mm. Named response at 3 mm or more: the edge is listed in the garments-step report and the implementing session widens the refit pose set for that vertex's bones; the picture decides whether the error shows.

**Shell regions (stage 1).** Predicates on `body['region']`:

| Shell | Region |
| --- | --- |
| trousers-waist | `y` in [hem, girdle], `arm` ≤ 0.2 |
| breastCloth-chest | `y` in [waist − 0.01 H, chestTop], `arm` ≤ 0.2 |
| neckBeads-chest | `y` in [chestTop − 0.06 H, neck − 0.008 H], `arm` ≤ 0.2 |
| neckBeads-neck | `y` in [neck − 0.008 H, neck + 0.002 H], `arm` ≤ 0.2 |
| veil-face | `y` in [neck − 0.025 H, chin + 0.055 H], whole head |
| waistBeads-waist | `y` in [hip + 0.013 H, hip + 0.027 H], `arm` ≤ 0.2 (B1) |

The trousers' region takes pelvis, seat, crotch and both legs by height, as the legacy seat tube did; the arms and the hanging hands are out by the arm scalar, which is 0 on hip and thigh skin. The waist beads band is the trunk loop between its two height cuts alone; the hand and wrist cross-sections at the same height carry arm scalar 1 and are out (B1).

**Shell builder (one recipe, two implementations).** Inputs: a body (positions, full uncut index, weights), the shell's face set, rank, base offset, and its boundary rows (the boundary loops of its own region face set and every higher-rank shell's boundary loops inside its region). Output: a geometry whose vertex k has a source index into the body. Positions are source plus offset along the body's area-weighted vertex normal, recomputed from the positions on the full index by `vertex_normals` in Python and the equivalent loop in TS. Each shell vertex carries its source's weights and skin indices, its source's normal as its own normal attribute, its source's `garmentPush` (the scalar from `decodeCover` with the legacy worn list, applied in the shader along `normalLocal`, so shell and source are pushed along identical skinned vectors and a rim foot never separates from pushed skin), zero relative morph targets of the shell's vertex count with the composed body's target names and `morphTargetsRelative = true` (as `gltfGarment` does; no shell reaches a hand, so zero deltas are exact), and in the pipeline additionally its source's morph deltas. A rim strip, a ring of quads from the offset row down to an un-offset copy of the same body vertices, is emitted at every boundary row that is a drawn edge: the shell's own rows always, a higher-rank shell's row only when that shell is worn and so cuts there. The rim strip is lit with the body's normal, one offset wide (3 to 12 mm), a recorded residual to look for in the pictures. The body's boundary vertices stay in the drawn faces outside the region. uv is cylindrical about the hips axis on the composed body (angle round the axis, height along it), so a fibre pattern keyed by uv keeps its scale. The shell passes through the same paint path as a legacy garment.

**Face cut, at assembly with the worn shell set and the legacy worn list.** A body triangle is cut if its region mask shares any bit with a worn shell, and independently by `maskedIndex` for the legacy mask. A shell triangle is cut if its source triangle's mask contains the bit of a worn shell of higher rank, or if all three of its source vertices are hidden by the worn legacy garments (revision 4 D7). Nothing is cut by a lower or equal rank.

**Data flow.**

- Pipeline: the garments step runs the recipe on the drawn body for the six and stores them under `garments['shells']`, outside `meshes`, so fit.py, mask.py and export.py's mesh loop never see them. pipeline.py's export branch passes `dress = {'meshes': ..., 'meta': ..., 'shells': garments.get('shells', {})}`. export.py writes no mesh for a shell and adds `extras.villager.shells`: bit names, per-face region masks, ranks, boundary rows, base offsets, and `kind: 'shell'` in `garments` meta for the six; the `g-` filter on the `meshes` loop is untouched and keeps hair, eyes and every shell out of the mesh list. The MK.check call after the export and the sheets step keep reading `garments` as before. cachekeys cover the shell module and the region scalars.
- Runtime: `gltfPerson` is unchanged apart from reading the shell extras. `gltfFigureGeometry` paints the body, decodes the legacy cover once with the legacy worn list, builds each worn shell from the painted body's positions on the full index, cuts body and shell faces by the rule above, sets each shell's push from the source flags, paints the shells, merges, and copies the relative flag and target names as today. The runtime reads the per-face masks and boundary rows from the extras and never classifies. One geometry and one draw call per figure.
- Round trip: villagerRuntimePose.test.tsx exports the composed rest body (after morph bake, hang, scale and `stylisePeaks`) and every worn shell's rest positions for the test outfits; gamepath.py recomputes the shells from the exported rest body and compares within 1e-4, then applies skinning and push to both and reads the posed shell-to-body clearance as a detector: the interior of a shell is expected within one offset step of its nominal offset, rims are reported separately and not held to it, and a reading outside the band names the frame, shell and vertex. Named response: inspect that frame in the picture; a reading under half the offset on more than one frame raises that shell's base offset by one step. A second comparison against gamepath's own drawn body with a `stylisePeaks` mirror is the detector of the full chain, as in revision 3.

**Migration of the six.** Each moves as a whole mesh in one commit; reference builders as listed in revision 3 (garments.py `build_form`). After migration the six are the regions of the table above at the ranks of the rank table; the breast cloth has no fill and follows the bust contour without bridging the cleft (recorded residual; named response: raise breastCloth's base offset by one step, and if the picture still reads as painted skin the breast cloth reverts to its legacy hull-tube builder and migrates with the trunk shells in stage 4). Bands are a rank step thick and read flat, a recorded residual. The legacy seat tube's hem at hip − 0.08 H is not needed: a skin-following shell has no gap between its seat and its legs. `finish`, `push_out`, weight transfer, `unskin` and `surface_map` are not called for these six. GARMENTS keeps the six entries so `build_form` output order is unchanged.

**Legacy garments over and under the shells.**
