B2 | docs/garment-merge-r2-1347.md | Tilt solver (prior B3): capsule clearance is not generally a lower bound on outward tilt; increasing tilt can move a panel into an outward-projecting bent limb. Ground clearance in a rotated hips frame is not universally monotone either, and neighbouring-column limits couple the solutions. Require solving actual feasible angle intervals or another bounded deterministic method, enforcing all constraints simultaneously rather than taking independent maxima.
B3 | docs/garment-merge-r2-1347.md | Infeasible attachments and ground (prior B3/B7): clamping a pelvis envelope smaller does not remove the actual body, and making a segment horizontal leaves its rendered points below ground when its head is below ground; horizontal also contradicts the 80-degree maximum. Reporting these failures and increasing ease or column count cannot repair an immovable attachment. Require explicit attachment exemptions against actual geometry and a feasible fallback for both segments and their rendered rows.
B4 | docs/garment-merge-r2-1347.md | Panel clearance (prior B4): the chord inequality can protect a checked edge, but it does not protect triangle interiors or unchecked diagonal edges. Two passes also do not establish that targets computed from preceding chord lengths hold after the final rotations. Require final skinned triangle–obstacle checks, including hips-blended panels, and a defined corrective response that re-verifies the resulting geometry.
B5 | docs/garment-merge-r2-1347.md | Collision-envelope containment (prior B5): containing body vertices in a union of capsules does not contain triangles spanning different capsules. Exported clip frames at body corners also exclude interpolated frames, intermediate morphs and the post-blending, stride-warped, leaned and IK-adjusted poses consumed by the driver. Require coverage of the relevant rendered surfaces over that actual pose domain, with envelope expansion or another explicit response when containment fails.
B6 | docs/garment-merge-r2-1347.md | Nested driven garments (prior B6): identical weights at corresponding vertices do not establish global surface ordering. A bound on the length of a transformed separation vector is not a bound on its outward normal component, and neighbouring vertices and rows use different affine maps. The 60-degree limit therefore does not prove the claimed 0.87 separation. Require actual inter-garment triangle checks and a correction strategy for crossings, rather than reporting them while retaining the ordering claim.
B7 | docs/garment-merge-r2-1347.md | Intermediate hold-at-rest routing (prior B12): freezing the new columns leaves a hips-driven cone while the legs continue walking or kneeling; this is not the previous garment deformation. The attached legacy builders also do not establish that outer garments were fitted against this new cone. Require migrating dependent combinations together or implementing and validating temporary routing against both moving limbs and legacy outer surfaces.
B8 | docs/garment-merge-r2-1347.md | Cloak arm clearance (new): retaining closed sides removes the earlier arm-escape assumption, but the replacement ruled panels follow chest/spine and the lower driver checks only pelvis, legs and feet. Arms can therefore cross the cloak without any construction or driver response. Require an explicit arm-clearance mechanism preserving the chosen opening, with posed surface checks for swing, carry and work motions.
B9 | docs/garment-merge-r2-1347.md | Junction ownership (prior B2/B17): a body-weighted girdle row does not move exactly like column heads parented to hips when that row has spine or thigh influence. The cloak additionally specifies hips-only weights at its bottom junction while the general junction rule requires body weights. Require one authoritative position, morph and weight definition for each shared boundary, including runtime reconstruction, and make the driver account for the boundary’s actual motion.
B10 | docs/garment-merge-r2-1347.md | Hem morph formulas (prior B8): adding hem heights to the row schedule does not give them their correct deltas. A hem at knee plus a constant must inherit the knee delta, whereas girdle–knee interpolation gives it some girdle delta; mid-thigh similarly depends on hip and knee, not girdle and knee. Require differentiating each supplied garment-specific hem formula, including separate front/back hems, and interpolating other rows through those resulting anchors.

Require suppressing pushes on protected shell regions and boundaries, or reconstructing against the composed body, with checks on the assembled result.
B12 | docs/garment-merge-r2-1347.md | Hull-fill construction (prior B9): ruling between body boundary curves does not specify the rank-offset shell boundary positions, so the fill can meet unoffset skin instead of the adjoining shell. Its new faces also lack a defined mapping into higher-shell face cuts, and interpolating positions and weights separately does not preserve the posed ruling merely because both endpoints have 0.9 chest influence. Require shared offset boundary vertices, explicit fill-face coverage rules and posed fill-clearance validation with a repair path.
B13 | docs/garment-merge-r2-1347.md | Outfit-dependent head geometry (new consequence of B13/B14): a shell’s offset now depends on which rigid head pieces are worn, contradicting the claim that outfit enters only the driver and face cut and that per-layer builds remain outfit-independent. The supplied layerOf callback receives one layer. Require applying these offsets to per-outfit clones during assembly or adding the relevant worn-piece set to construction inputs and cache keys.
B14 | docs/garment-merge-r2-1347.md | Head-envelope layering (prior B14, new offset rule): max(rankOffset, pieceEnvelope + step) gives different-ranked shells exactly the same offset wherever the piece envelope dominates, eliminating their separation. A per-body-vertex envelope also does not by itself protect intervening shell triangles or establish clearance between retained rigid pieces. Require rank-preserving offsets above the envelope and actual surface-clearance checks for every reachable layered head arrangement.
B15 | docs/garment-merge-r2-1347.md | Sling exterior placement (prior B15): the supplied sling standoff is measured from a body outline, not the revised cloak, and spine1 motion differs from the cloak’s chest/spine blend and driven panels. Declaring it outside and adding a contact report supplies no way to keep it outside when contact occurs. Require a validated exterior envelope or an outfit/pose-dependent placement response, preserving the sling’s rigid shape.
B16 | docs/garment-merge-r2-1347.md | Limb-ring finishing (prior B15): assigning forearm/shin weights and declaring rings inside garments does not establish their fit to skin or covering cloth. The retained builder uses fixed major radii, its existing morph/clearance path disappears with finish, and finish_rigid is specified as head-only. Require a complete limb-ring morph and finishing contract, checking skin and covering garments together and defining how failed inside-placement checks are corrected.
B17 | docs/garment-merge-r2-1347.md | Waist-bead ordering (new): the fixed rank-3 band cannot be inside every hip garment under the stated rank rule because trousers are rank 2. At their overlapping waist region, the face-cut rule removes trouser faces beneath the beads, making the beads the exterior surface. Require a consistent ordering and cutting policy covering tight trousers as well as driven hip garments.
B18 | docs/garment-merge-r2-1347.md | Computed pivots through runtime hang (prior B2, new integration defect): column knee pivots are computed in the body step, but the supplied hungHeads moves real shin joints when thighs are hung while hips-parented columns retain their original heights. Thus the new pivots need not coincide with the drawn body’s knee landmarks even before animation. Require recomputing or consistently transforming computed pivots and row anchors through the actual hang, grounding and scaling chain, with runtime equality checks.
B19 | docs/garment-merge-r2-1347.md | Partial-panel angular sampling (new): the twelve-column replacement specifies no separate cloth angular grid or inserted flap boundaries. If it retains the supplied sweep/keep construction at twelve samples, the loin-flap and girdle-tail front interval abs(angle) <= 0.5 contains only angle zero, so no front quad survives. Require explicit boundary insertion or a sufficiently detailed cloth grid independent of the twelve driver columns, preserving partial-panel widths and openings.
B20 | docs/garment-merge-r2-1347.md | Driven flare and revised ranks (new): flare is defined only for ranks 1, 2 and 4, although rank-6 cloaks have driven panels and step 1 may introduce further ranks to resolve overlaps. Their rest-radius construction is therefore incomplete, and rank changes can silently change garment shape. Require explicit flare/ease definitions for every driven form, with ordering rules that remain valid after rank splits.
