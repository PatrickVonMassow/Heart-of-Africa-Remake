# Villager garment construction — merged plan, revision 1 (Fable 5.1)

Answers GPT-6 Astra's round-1 break check (docs/garment-check-1-1347.md) and replaces the plan in docs/garment-merge-1347.md.

I'll check each of GPT-6 Astra's 22 objections against the attached pipeline, export, skeleton and runtime code, then rewrite the merged plan and list the defects I can point at.

## 1. Objection answers

- **B1** FIXED. `sweep`, `torus`, `blob` and a one-shot `push_out` are kept for the rigid head pieces; `tube` is deleted in step 4 after its last caller (the wraps) migrates, not in step 2; the pipeline's garments step calls the new builder from step 2 on, with `fit.tailor` reduced to the still-legacy forms until step 6, where its import and selftest call go with the file.
- **B2** FIXED. Column bones are appended after the 22 existing `BONES`, so existing indices are stable; body joints, joint deltas, the full and top-4 weight arrays and every clip's quaternion array are extended compatibly in one skeleton step; skeleton.py enters the body, clips and garments cache keys so the pickles rebuild.
- **B3** FIXED. The knee skirt migrates in step 3 with the short skirt, with kneel and dig acceptance shots for its wearers.
- **B4** FIXED. The hair bag stops being "as built": it becomes a rank-0 scalp shell patch thickened outward, since garments.py starts it 4 cm inside the skull for `fit.settle` to push out. Turban, cap, headband, head ring and topknot keep their builders plus a rest-pose clearance pass that no longer imports from fit.py, with `inside` moved out of fit.py.
- **B5** FIXED. The body itself is pre-split along every garment iso-line in the body step, so every shell vertex, boundary included, is a body vertex with the body's own four weights. Correspondence is then exact by identity, not by interpolation.
- **B6** FIXED. The same pre-split gives compatible boundaries. Each body triangle carries a region bitmask recorded at build, exported with the face sets, so "inside" is a per-face fact and nothing is partially covered.
- **B7** FIXED. Shells carry a source-index attribute. At runtime a shell vertex is rebuilt from the drawn body vertex after morph bake, hang, scale and `stylisePeaks`, plus its offset. Export precision no longer matters for shell positions or weights. A Vitest test checks shell equals body plus offset after the full figure build.
- **B8** FIXED. Column rows take morph deltas interpolated between the girdle-ring vertex and the hem landmark joint (knee or ankle delta from `jointDeltas`), per row fraction, so length and the knee segment split follow the morphed leg.
- **B9** FIXED. Column pivots are ordinary skeleton bones with heads in `body['joints']` and deltas in `jointDeltas`, so `restHeads`, hang, grounding and scale produce them before `gltfGarment` bakes and `createGltfSkeleton` binds. The "derived later in the driver" wording is withdrawn.
- **B10** FIXED. The junction row is one shared vertex row with the body's weights. Hips-plus-column weighting starts at the next row down. No seam can split because no vertex is duplicated.
- **B11** FIXED. The driver's clearance adds the chord sagitta of the twelve-gon at the leg radius, and penetration.py re-pointed at the driven pose measures the actual skinned panels in walk, kneel and dig as an acceptance input, from step 3.
- **B12** FIXED. Enclosing per-segment radii are measured from the drawn body (max distance of a segment's dominant vertices from its bone line at three stations), stored on the person, and the driver clears every column segment against both thighs, both shins, a pelvis capsule and both feet.
- **B13** FIXED. One driver per figure takes the outfit as input: clearance is leg radius plus every worn thigh-shell offset plus the largest ease among worn driven garments. Nested panels share one rotation and keep their rank radii, which preserves order. Apron with loin flap, wrap with robe, robe with cloak go into acceptance.
- **B14** FIXED. Intermediate contract for steps 2 to 5: shells export no cover attribute and are absent from the mask bit list, the body under a shell is cut by the new face set, legacy garments keep their geometry-computed masks over the body, and fit.py receives shells as fixed inner layers to settle clear of.
- **B15** FIXED. The waist-bead arrangement is settled against the design reference in step 1, and the beads migrate in step 3 as a band welded to the outermost worn hip garment's columns when one is worn, rank 3 over bare skin otherwise. The "cut away under a robe" claim is withdrawn: at hip plus 0.02 H the beads lie below the girdle, outside the robe's shell region.
- **B16** FIXED. The rank table is keyed by form and wear, the mesh name's own identity, and gains rows for every retained head piece and the hood-over-hair-bag case.
- **B17** FIXED. The chest wrap's shell region runs from the chest-top iso-line to the girdle with arms excluded and hull fill over the bust, matching the strapless `tube` the builder uses today. Only shirt and robe include upper arms.
- **B18** FIXED. Per-piece policy: the yoke shell cuts like any rank-6 shell, the ruled upper panels and the driven lower panels cut nothing.
- **B19** FIXED. The driver clamps a lower segment that would pass below the ground to lie along it, and acceptance adds low-camera kneel and dig shots of the hem.
- **B20** FIXED. A surviving dark mark is rendered with a debug unlit material and back-face colouring on both backends and traced to its mesh before it is classified.
- **B21** FIXED. The round-trip payload gains the worn list, the driven column world rotations and sampled skinned panel positions per frame, compared within the same tolerance.
- **B22** FIXED. Acceptance adds slight, stout and intermediate builds, shoulder and head extremes, carry and kneel transitions, and coverage checks for sole-cloak and sole-upper-cape outfits. The build-0 limitation of the sheets is taken from the check, since the inventory was not supplied here.

## 2. Revised merged plan

**Diagnosis.** A's four mechanisms stand as the working diagnosis: hull tubes across both legs blended between the thighs, the rest shape inflated to the union of all poses, weights transferred from the nearest body point, and a pose-independent mask that turns each cloth error into a hole. B could not see the clip, rig or pose files and rightly calls the cause of the walking-trouser report unproven. The merged plan keeps A's hypothesis for report (1) as a hypothesis and makes the in-game walking trouser shot the test, which A also demands. B's two code findings that A did not report are accepted on B's reading of files it did see: the pipeline still raises on penetration over tolerance, which contradicts point 1339 and goes first; and the runtime hide-bit union can delete a triangle no single garment covers, which A's exact face sets remove anyway.

**Tight garments** are body shells. Trousers, the trunk of shirt and robe with upper arms, the trunk of toga and chest wrap without arms, the breast cloth, the hip, girdle and loin-flap bands, the neck ring, the covering chest beads, the veil and the hair bag are copies of the drawn body's region. The body mesh is pre-split in the body step along every garment iso-line, so each region boundary is a body vertex row and a shell contains no vertex the body lacks. Each body triangle carries a region bitmask recorded at build. A shell vertex carries a source index into the body. At runtime it is rebuilt from the drawn body vertex after morph bake, hang, grounding, scale and `stylisePeaks`, offset outward along the body normal by the garment's rank; weights are copied from the body's expanded skin attributes. The pipeline builds the same from its body for sheets and penetration. No unposing, transfer or smoothing exists, and export precision does not enter shell positions. The chest wrap keeps its present strapless top at the chest-top iso-line with hull fill over the bust on the chest bone; the breast cloth likewise. Hull fill is allowed only where the region is weighted at least 0.9 to one trunk bone. Cuffs and necklines keep the body's boundary row under a rim strip. Up to about 1 cm of ease on convex parts tapers to the base offset at the armpit, crotch and back of the knee. B's warning that this reads bra-like or painted on is recorded as a residual to judge in the picture.

**Loose garments** hang from a driven skirt rig, and B's runtime cloth solver is rejected. Everything below the girdle line, meaning the short and knee skirts, both long wraps, the robe, shirt and toga hems, loin-flap and apron panels, girdle tails, waist beads over a hip garment, and the hanging panels of cloaks and capes, is a set of columns joined to the bottom ring of the garment's shell or band. Twelve columns of two segments each are real skeleton bones appended after the existing 22, parented to hips, with heads in the body joints and deltas in the joint deltas, so the runtime's `restHeads`, hang and scale place them before baking and binding. The junction row is the shell's bottom row with the body's weights; from the next row down a vertex is weighted to hips and its two nearest columns by angle, never to a thigh or shin. Each row's morph delta interpolates between its girdle-ring vertex delta and the hem landmark joint delta, so skirt length and the knee split follow a child's or stout body. One stateless driver per figure per frame, after clip blending, stride warping, lean and IK, takes the worn list as input. It rotates each column segment until it clears both thighs, both shins, a pelvis capsule and both feet by the enclosing segment radius measured from that person's drawn body, plus the chord sagitta of the twelve-gon, plus the offsets of every worn thigh-covering shell, plus the largest ease among the worn driven garments. The lower segment blends toward gravity and is clamped to lie along the ground where it would pass below it. Nested panels share the one rotation and keep their rank radii, so they stay ordered. The driver runs identically at every quality level and both backends. A Python mirror lives in the game-path module. The runtime round trip carries the worn list, the driven column world rotations and sampled skinned panel positions, compared within the existing tolerance. Penetration.py measures the driven pose from the step the driver lands. Normals come from the same linear blend skinning.

**Head pieces and ornaments.** The hood keeps its rigid head part and gets a shoulder shell blending from head through neck into body weights. The veil is a shell over the lower face and the whole neck that continues about 4 cm under the robe or shirt collar and under the cloak yoke, with the overlapped part cut. Whether the Tuareg heads still read as uniform dark at 3 m is checked after the rebuild as a colour and material question. The chest beads of the Zulu youth and Mongo women stay an opaque covering shell. The neck ring and girdle band become thin shell bands. Waist beads are a band on the outermost worn hip garment's columns when a skirt, wrap, robe or flap is worn, and a rank-3 band over skin otherwise, subject to the step-1 dress decision. Limb rings are rigid tori on the forearm and shin segments, sized per body. The baby sling stays rigid on spine1 at mid-back. The hair bag becomes a scalp shell patch thickened outward, because today it starts inside the skull for the settle to push it out. Turban, cap, headband, head ring, topknot, hair cap and eyes keep their builders with a one-shot rest-pose clearance that no longer depends on fit.py.

**Cloaks and capes.** The both-shoulder and chest cloaks become a shoulder yoke shell plus a back panel and two front panels open at the sides, the upper part a ruled surface on chest and spine past the bust, the lower part on the skirt columns. The yoke shell cuts like any rank-6 shell; the ruled and driven panels cut nothing. The hanging arm swings in the open side. The chest cape is a shoulder shell to above the elbow with a dropped hem lip. The right-shoulder cape is a diagonal sash shell plus a short back panel. The left-shoulder cape reuses the toga builder at knee length.

**Layering** is one explicit offset rank per form and wear, replacing the per-slot table:

| --- | --- |
| 0 | neckBeads-neck, neckBeads-chest, hairBag-crown, headRing-crown, topknot-crown, cap-crown, headband-aroundHead, hair cap |

Limb rings and the sling are rigid on top of whatever they touch. Rigid head pieces neither cut nor are cut; the hood's rigid part keeps its ease over them, and hood over hair bag is an acceptance case. The offset is about 3 mm base plus 3 mm per rank, calibratable. Body faces and inner-shell faces whose region bitmask lies inside a higher-rank worn shell's region are cut from the index. Nothing is hidden under a driven panel. The build check is "two worn garments of the same rank with overlapping regions".

**Outfit restrictions.** None. The rank order and the shared, outfit-aware columns resolve every pair, including hood under cloak in the Tuareg rows and sling over the chest cloak. The generator keeps the open-outer rule of §19.15 as today.

**Expected residuals.** Inherited body skinning pinch at the knee in kneel and the hip crease in dig. Shell self-intersection inside deep creases. A crease at the front corners of a skirt between two columns with very different rotations, with sixteen columns as the fallback. A small thigh-top contact under the girdle ring in dig. A lifted child's flap showing the inner thigh. Trousers reading tighter than period cloth and wide Hausa trousers losing volume, OPEN. The breast cloth reading bra-like, OPEN. Fitted-to-loose seams and the shell edge rows on the child body possibly faceted. Hems lying along the ground in kneel and dig, judged in low-camera pictures. A surviving dark mark is traced to its mesh with a debug unlit material and back-face colouring on both backends before it is classified; none is accepted as shading untraced.

**Cost.** Pipeline time falls from the fit, settle and mask passes to a closed-form build; measured after cutover. Runtime stays one merged geometry and one draw call per figure with 24 extra bones and about 24 segments times a handful of capsule tests per figure per frame on the CPU. Draw count and crowd frame time are checked in both backends in the village scene after cutover. Effort is the body pre-split, one shell builder, one skirt builder, the driver with its mirror and tests, and the skeleton and export extension.

**Migration.** Removed, each in the step that makes it dead: `tube`, `column_sweep`, `sleeve`, the weight transfer and smoothing, `skirt_weights`, `unskin`, `armholes`, `OUTER` and `SKIRTS`, all of fit.py with its self tests, mask.py's volume, motion, decision and push classes, the garment push and the push half of the cover table in the runtime mask, the asset values only those read, the per-slot layer table, and the pipeline's penetration exception. Kept: body, MakeHuman body, rig, clips, gltfio, export, the game path, sheets and inventory, `sweep`, `torus` and `blob` for the rigid head pieces, a one-shot `push_out` with `inside` moved out of fit.py, and penetration.py re-pointed at the driven pose. Intermediate contract for steps 2 to 5: shells export no cover attribute and are not in the mask garment list, the body under a shell is cut by the new face set, legacy garments keep their masks over the body, fit.py takes shells as fixed inner layers, and a legacy outer garment over a shell relies on the settle alone. A form-and-wear mesh moves in one step, never half of it.

**Implementation order, each step landable on its own:**

1. Remove the penetration exception and stale gate text. Record the before pictures per outfit. Settle the waist-bead wearing arrangement against the design reference. Pre-split the body along the garment iso-lines with per-face region bitmasks, and confirm the sheets are unchanged.
2. Skeleton extension: append the 24 column bones, extend joints, joint deltas, weights and clip quaternions compatibly, add skeleton.py to the body, clips and garments cache keys, export them as ordinary bones. Shell builder with the source-index contract, rank table by form and wear, face-set cut. The pipeline garments step calls the new builder, with `fit.tailor` left for legacy forms. Migrate trousers, breast cloth, veil, both neck-bead forms and chest wrap top? No: migrate only whole meshes without a driven part: trousers, breastCloth, veil, neckBeads-neck, neckBeads-chest. Acceptance: forced walking and running trouser wearers side-on at 3 m and 7 m on both backends; shell-equals-body test.
3. The driver in TypeScript after IK, with outfit input, enclosing radii, sagitta and ground clamp; its Python mirror; the extended round trip; penetration.py re-pointed; the Vitest envelope tests. Migrate skirtShort, skirtKnee, loinFlap, apron, girdleTails and waistBeads as whole meshes. Acceptance: children in walk and run, knee-skirt wearers in kneel and dig, apron with loin flap.
4. Trunk shells plus driven hems for shirt, robe, both togas, both wraps and the left-shoulder cape. Delete `tube`, `sleeve`, `skirt_weights`, `armholes`. Acceptance: every robe, toga and wrap wearer in walk, sprint, kneel and dig; wrap under robe.
5. Cloaks, chest and right-shoulder capes, hood shoulder shell, sling move, limb rings rigid, hair bag as scalp patch, the one-shot clearance for the other head pieces. Delete `column_sweep`, `OUTER`, the per-slot layer table. Acceptance: Tuareg, San, Maasai and Zulu rows; robe under cloak; hood over hair bag; sole-cloak and sole-upper-cape coverage.
6. Delete fit.py with the pipeline's import and selftest call, the mask classes, the runtime push, the dead asset values and cache keys; move the calibratable values to balance config; update the design reference record; shoot the after pictures and the 158-outfit sheets plus slight, stout and intermediate builds, shoulder and head extremes, carry and kneel transitions; trace mesh ownership for whatever of the bambara detached pieces and Hausa spots survives.

**Disagreements resolved.** Unchanged from the previous plan except: A26 vs B49 now reads "column bones are skeleton bones with joint deltas; ranks and face sets in the glb extras; no versioned schema"; A §3 morphs vs B48 now reads "rows interpolate girdle and hem landmark deltas". The other eighteen entries stand as written.
