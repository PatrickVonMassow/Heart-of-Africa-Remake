# Round-2 objections — merger's audit (Fable 5.1), not yet a revised plan

Asked for revision 2 of docs/garment-merge-r1-1347.md against docs/garment-check-2-1347.md; Fable 5.1 answered with an audit of the objections instead. Next step: commission the revised plan (r2) from it, then Astra round 3.

I'll read the round-2 objections and the revised plan against the attached pipeline, export, skeleton and runtime code, then list only defects I can point at a line for.

Checked: every B1..B18 objection against garments.py, export.py, skeleton.py, pipeline.py and villagerFigureBody.ts, and the revised plan's prose, rank table, migration list and implementation order against the same code. Sixteen of the eighteen objections hold on the code as supplied. One objection (B11) asserts a break it does not demonstrate. The plan itself has several defects the check did not raise.

A1 | docs/garment-check-2-1347.md | B11 claims body/shell correspondence "can immediately diverge" into a defect, but shells are absent from the mask list and the only supplied effect of a worn legacy garment's mask on the body is a push away from that garment (villagerFigureBody.ts `applyGarmentMask(tidy(body), person.cover, asset.garmentMask, worn, depth)`); the objection shows no inward-pushed body ending up outside a shell, only that the plan's "shell equals body plus offset" Vitest test (B7 answer) must run on the unmasked body.
A2 | docs/garment-merge-r1-1347.md | Step 2 contains editing residue, "Migrate trousers, breast cloth, veil, both neck-bead forms and chest wrap top? No: migrate only whole meshes...", a self-question left inside the plan text that a session acting on it cannot execute.
A3 | docs/garment-merge-r1-1347.md | Waist-bead hosts "when a skirt, wrap, robe or flap is worn" omit shirt and toga, whose driven hems reach knee+0.02H and ankle+0.03H in garments.py (`bottom = L['knee'] + 0.02 * H if form == 'shirt' else L['ankle'] + 0.03 * H`); the beads at hip+0.02H then become a rank-3 skin band under a driven panel, which "nothing is hidden under a driven panel" and a driver clearance counting only "thigh-covering shells" never clear.
A4 | docs/garment-merge-r1-1347.md | "The hood keeps its rigid head part": garments.py has no rigid hood part; `hood` is not in RIGID_HEAD and is finished with `smooth=2` weight transfer (`return sweep(rings, closed_bottom=True, ...), 2`), so there is nothing to keep and the head-to-shell junction B17 describes is new construction.
A5 | docs/garment-merge-r1-1347.md | "Toga ... without arms" contradicts the builder: above chestTop the toga's outline is `yoke = trunk & (B.lower_arm < 0.3)`, which includes the covered shoulder's upper-arm cap, so a shell region excluding arms uncovers that shoulder cap, which the plan presents as preserving the garment.
A6 | docs/garment-merge-r1-1347.md | "The left-shoulder cape reuses the toga builder at knee length" silently lengthens the cape: garments.py sets every cape's bottom at `L['waist'] - 0.02 * H` (just under the chest joint), and the plan records no design decision to change it.
A7 | docs/garment-merge-r1-1347.md | Column bones "with heads in body joints and deltas in jointDeltas" has no definition of those heads or deltas: skeleton.py's BONES schema requires a MakeHuman joint group per head and tail ("name, parent, MakeHuman joint group of the head, joint group (or None) of the tail") and export.py reads `body['joints']` and `body['joint_deltas']` for every SK.NAMES entry, so the skeleton step cannot be landed as written.
A8 | docs/garment-merge-r1-1347.md | Step 1 "Pre-split the body ... and confirm the sheets are unchanged" cannot hold: export.py reduces every vertex to four weights (`jidx, jw = top4(W)`), so an iso-line vertex interpolated between two body vertices with different bone sets is truncated and skins off the edge it replaced; the plan should budget a tolerance, as its own B10 answer will have to.
A9 | docs/garment-merge-r1-1347.md | Rank table row 0 lists "hair cap", which is not a form-and-wear garment and never reaches the game: pipeline.py exports only meshes named `g-*` and says "The hair cap and the eyes stay pipeline-only (the game paints the scalp)", so the table's "keyed by form and wear" claim (B16 answer) is broken by its own row.
A10 | docs/garment-merge-r1-1347.md | Head-pieces paragraph gives "eyes" a one-shot rest-pose clearance, but `eyes()` in garments.py is the base mesh's eyeballs rigid on the head with no push or fit at all; a clearance pass would move them out of the sockets.
